Autonomous Quadcopter Drone Project

Welcome to my hobby project page! Here I showcase the specifications, flight gallery, and video demonstration of my custom drone.

Drone Specifications

Here are the primary components and features of the quadcopter:

  • Frame: F450 Quadcopter Frame
  • Motors: 1000KV Brushless DC Motors
  • ESC: 30A Electronic Speed Controllers
  • Flight Controller: Pixhawk 2.4.8 / APM
  • Battery: 3S 2200mAh LiPo Battery
  • Battery Life / Flight Time: 12–15 minutes

Key Assembly Steps

  1. Mount the ESCs and motors to the quadcopter frame arms.
  2. Connect the Flight Controller and calibrate the IMU and compass.
  3. Bind the RC Transmitter and Receiver.
  4. Perform a ground test before mounting the propellers.
